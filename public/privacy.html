Portdle

Privacy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Playing without an account

Signing in is optional. Without an account, Daily, Blitz, History, and the Codex keep their data only in your browser (its local storage).

What signing in stores

You can sign in with Google or GitHub. Portdle then stores the email of your account. It uses the email only to sign you in and to link a Google and a GitHub sign-in that share the same verified email. The email is never shown to other players.

Portdle also stores a display name. It is public. It starts as your GitHub username or your Google account name, and you confirm or change it before it is public.

What syncs, and who can see it

When you are signed in, your Codex, Blitz Bests, Blitz runs, Dailies, and preferences (theme, How-to seen flag, Codex sort) sync to your account. Other players cannot see them.

What is public

Your Blitz leaderboard rows are public: display name, score, time to score, and rank.

Where the data is

Supabase hosts the data, in the São Paulo region.

What Portdle does not do

There are no ads, no tracking, and no selling of data.

Deleting your account and local data

Choose Delete account in the account menu. It removes your account and all its data, leaderboard rows included, and clears this device.

Any local copy of your data stays in that browser, on this device or another, until you clear the site's data in your browser settings. Signing out also keeps it.
