# Ticket spec: Late mark and stats from records

Spec for #189. Tracker: https://github.com/mquesada02/portdle/issues/189. Parent: Spec 5 (Dailies from the backend), https://github.com/mquesada02/portdle/issues/181 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-spec5-dailies-backend.md`, "Daily records, `late`, and stats", "Backend schema", `dailies.late`). Map: https://github.com/mquesada02/portdle/issues/169. Base branch: `spec5`.

## Problem Statement

Stats are stored (`stats:*`, `lastPlayed:*`, `lastSeenUtc`) and patched step by step: on each Daily finish, on each device-clock day change in the app shell, and after each sync pull. Stored stats can drift from the saved Dailies, and they cannot tell an on-day play from a late play. A Daily record has no `late` mark, so a play that starts after its day cannot be kept out of streaks, and two devices cannot agree on which plays were late.

## Solution

A Daily record carries `late`, set once when its first try is committed. Stats are never stored: every screen folds them from the saved records with the server today. The fold counts a late record in totals only. Sync carries `late` and merges it as "on-day wins". The `dailies.late` migration lands in the backend. A local switch-over step runs at every app start.

The Archive (where a late play happens) is #190. Until then the Daily screen plays only today's Daily, so its records are on-day. The engine rule for `late` ships now, because the record shape, the merge, the fold, and the database column all depend on it.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want my stats rebuilt from my saved Dailies every time, so that they never differ from my record (spec stories 49, 51).
2. As a player with an unfinished on-day Daily, I want my current streak to count as broken until I win it, and to join the days around it once I do (story 50).
3. As a player, I want a late play to count in Played, Win %, and the histogram, and never to extend, repair, or break a streak (stories 44, 45).
4. As a player, I want a Daily I started on its day to stay on-day when I finish it after midnight (story 13).
5. As a signed-out player, I want my records to keep their `late` mark and sync it at sign-in (story 55).
6. As a player on two devices, I want the on-day mark to win when one device played on-day and the other late (story 56).
7. As a returning player, I want my saved Dailies on or before the switch-over day kept and marked on-day, and records after it that have no mark discarded (stories 52, 53).
8. As the owner, I want the backend to hold `late`, delete the rows after the switch-over day, and reject an unmarked row after it (stories 54, 55).

## Implementation Decisions

### The record and the late rule

- `DailyPersist` gains `late?: boolean`. An absent value reads as on-day everywhere (a record saved before this change, or a pulled null).
- `src/engine/utc.ts` gets `isLate(date, serverToday): boolean` = `serverToday > date` (ISO dates compare as strings, as `utc.ts` does elsewhere). In this spec "server today" is the `today` of the current Dailies; the Daily screen already receives it as its `utcToday` prop. The device-clock `utcToday` of `RouterContext` is a different value and no code in this ticket reads it.
- When the Daily screen commits the first try of a record (there is no stored record), the new record carries `late: isLate(date, utcToday)`, where `utcToday` is the server today the screen already uses. A later try keeps the stored `late` unchanged; the stored `late` is never rewritten. `applyCommittedGuess` (`src/engine/guess.ts`) builds a new record from scratch and would drop the mark, so it gains a third parameter `late: boolean`: when `current` is null the new record carries it; otherwise the new record carries `current.late` (an absent `late` stays absent). The screen passes `isLate(date, utcToday)`; the engine does not know the date.
- `isDailyPersist` in `src/persist/storage.ts` accepts a record whose `late` is absent or a boolean, and refuses any other `late`.

### Merge

- `mergeDaily` still picks the record with the existing rule. The result's `late` is set separately: `true` only when both sides are `true`; absent only when both sides are absent; otherwise `false`. A side with an absent `late` counts as on-day. The result is commutative and idempotent (not associative, which sync does not need: it merges pairwise). The picked record's other fields are untouched.

### Fold

- `foldStats({ dailies, serverToday })` moves to `src/engine/stats.ts` and replaces `foldStats` in `merge.ts`, `applyStreakGap`, and `applyDailyFinish`. `dailies` maps date to record for one category. It returns `CategoryStats`.
- Records dated after `serverToday` are ignored by the whole fold.
- Played, wins, and the histogram come from every finished record (`won` or `lost`), late or not.
- Streaks use only on-day records (`late !== true`). A date is a "win day" when its on-day record is `won`. Best streak is the longest run of consecutive win days. Current streak: the run of consecutive win days ending today when today is a win day; `0` when today holds a finished on-day loss; otherwise the run ending yesterday. A day with no on-day record, an unfinished on-day record before `serverToday`, an on-day loss, and a day with only a late record are all non-win days, so they end a run. Today's unfinished on-day Daily does not break the current streak.
- A helper in `src/ui/category-stats.ts` builds the date-to-record map of one category through `persist.listDailyDates` and `persist.readDaily` (the loop that `rebuildStats` in `sync.ts` holds today, which goes away) and returns the fold at the server today. The Daily screen calls it where it now calls `readStats`, after the finishing record is written (it is recomputed on every render, never memoised on `board`); the per-render cost is the old stats read plus a scan of one category's keys, and is accepted. It replaces `src/ui/sync-utc.ts`, which is deleted.

### Stored stats removed

- `Persist` loses `readStats`, `writeStats`, `readLastPlayed`, `writeLastPlayed`, `readLastSeenUtc`, `writeLastSeenUtc`, their key helpers, `isCategoryStats`, and the `stats`, `lastPlayed`, and `lastSeenUtc` kinds of `PersistChange`. `CategoryStats` stays as the fold's output type.
- `src/ui/daily-page.tsx`: delete the `readStats` call, the `categoryStreakGap` and `sync-utc` imports, and the finish block that calls `readLastPlayed`, `writeStats`, `writeLastPlayed`, and `writeLastSeenUtc`; the reveal's stats come from the fold at the server today, read after the finishing record is written, so a just-finished Daily counts. The `finishedThisSession` flag and the Codex writes stay. `src/ui/daily-reveal.tsx` keeps its `stats` prop (its type and look do not change). `src/ui/history-page.tsx`, `src/router.tsx`, and `src/ui/leaderboards.tsx` hold no stats reader; the implementer confirms with a search for each removed symbol.
- `applySwitchOver` is a one-time migration step. Ticket #194 (release) deletes it with the placeholder cleanup if the owner agrees; this ticket only adds it.
- `src/ui/shell.tsx`: ticket #188 owns deleting the `syncUtcDay` effect, the shell's `utcToday` destructure, the device-clock state in `App`, and `RouterContext.utcToday` (owner decision for #188). Removing `readStats` and the other accessors breaks that effect's compile, so if #188 has not merged into `spec5` when this PR opens, this ticket deletes only the effect, its `sync-utc` import, and the `utcToday` destructure (owner-approved minimal deletion); the second merge resolves trivially. If #188 has merged, nothing in the shell changes. After the deletion, the implementer checks that `useEffect` and `setSaveWarning` are still used, so lint passes.
- `createSync` loses the `utcToday` option, `rebuildStats`, and the `changedCategories` set. This ticket also deletes `App`'s optional `utcToday` prop and `utcTodayRef` (owner decision for #188: #188 keeps them and #189 deletes them with the stored stats), and any import left unused (`useRef`, `utcDateString`, `DailyCategory`, `DailyPersist`). If #188 has not merged, `App` keeps the device-clock state that reads the prop, so the prop is deleted only once nothing reads it; the implementer raises any conflict with the coordinator. Tests that pass `utcToday` to `App` or `createSync` are updated.

### Sync

- `DailyRow` (`src/remote/rows.ts`) gains `late: boolean | null`. `toRows` sends `late: daily.late ?? null`. `fromRows` sets `late` on the record only when the row's value is not null. The field order stays `status`, `guesses`, `late`; sync compares with sorted keys, so a record from the database compares equal to the local copy.
- Merge on pull uses `mergeDaily`, which now carries `late` as above. A record that differs from the local copy only in `late` is written, because the comparison is on the whole record; the write is done under sync's `writing` flag, as every pull write is, and the next push marking compares with the account snapshot.

### Local switch-over

- `Persist` gains `applySwitchOver(switchOverDay: string): { ok: true } | { ok: false }` (the `Persist` write convention; `main.tsx` ignores a failure, since the step is idempotent and runs again at the next start), implemented in `storage.ts` where the keys are private. It: removes every `portdle:v1:stats:*`, `portdle:v1:lastPlayed:*`, and `portdle:v1:lastSeenUtc` key; for each stored valid Daily record with no `late`, dated on or before `switchOverDay`, writes it back with `late: false`; for each such record dated after it, removes the record and its `category|date` entry in the pending `dailies` list of the sync bookkeeping. A record with a `late` value, and a stored value that is not a valid record, are left alone. Running it twice changes nothing more than running it once.
- `src/main.tsx` calls `persist.applySwitchOver(SWITCH_OVER_DAY)` once at start, before the first render, so no screen mounts on a record the step will remove or change. Local keys keep the `portdle:v1` prefix. The step does not mark anything for push itself: at start no sync listener is attached. After each pull, sync marks every local record that differs from the account's copy (`mark(kind, localRows(kind))` in `syncOnce`), so a rewritten record is pushed at the next sync. A removal of a discarded record's pending entry is a direct write to the sync bookkeeping, using the same `category|date` key as `dailyKey` in `src/engine/daily-key.ts`.
- `SWITCH_OVER_DAY` is still a placeholder until the release (`src/engine/switch-over.ts`). While it holds `2026-10-01`, a dev's saved unmarked records dated after it are deleted on first load of this build. The spec accepts this.

### Backend

- New migration `supabase/migrations/20261001000012_dailies_late.sql`, to apply in the SQL editor after `20261001000011`, on the switch-over day and never earlier (this holds for the live project; `supabase start` applies every file in the folder, so the local stack and CI apply it at once, which is why the test drops the column and constraint first):
  - `alter table public.dailies add column late boolean;` (nullable, no default);
  - delete rows with `day > public.switch_over_day()`;
  - `update public.dailies set late = false` on the rest;
  - a named `CHECK` constraint `dailies_late_required`: `late is not null or day <= public.switch_over_day()`;
  - `grant execute on function public.switch_over_day() to authenticated;`. The import migration revoked execute from `authenticated`, and a `CHECK` runs as the writing role, so without this grant every signed-in push fails with `permission denied`. The function returns a public constant; `anon` stays revoked (owner decision, 2026-10-02).
  - It calls `public.switch_over_day()`, which the generated import migration already holds from the one constant, so it needs no regeneration. The file holds no `begin` or `commit`, so the test can roll it back.
- New SQL test `supabase/tests/dailies-late.sql` (psql only, run by CI through `scripts/run-sql-tests.sh`, which runs from the repository root; `catalog-entries.sql` is the precedent for a top-level `begin` and `rollback`, `account-deletion.sql` for acting as `authenticated` with a JWT claim). A first transaction (rolled back) creates an `auth.users` row (the account row comes from the existing seed trigger, as in `row-sync.sql`), drops `dailies_late_required` and the `late` column, inserts rows before and after the switch-over day, runs the real migration with `\i supabase/migrations/20261001000012_dailies_late.sql`, and asserts: rows after the day are gone, the rest have `late = false`, a null `late` after the day is rejected with `check_violation`, a null `late` on or before the day is accepted, and `true` and `false` are accepted on any day. In the same transaction, after the migration, it sets the role `authenticated` with the user's id as the JWT subject and inserts and updates a `dailies` row with a `late` value, to prove the `CHECK` runs for that role. A second transaction on the untouched schema asserts the column is a nullable boolean with no default. It leaves no rows behind.

## Testing Decisions

Tests assert behavior through the highest seam: the fold's numbers, the local store's contents, the fake account's rows, and what the Reveal shows.

- `stats.test.ts`: the fold (totals include late records; late records never extend, repair, or break a streak; a day with only a late record is a gap; an unfinished on-day record before server today breaks the streak until it is won, and joins the days around it once won; today's unfinished Daily does not break; a finished loss today gives 0; a record after server today is ignored; empty input). The old `applyStreakGap` and `applyDailyFinish` tests go with the functions; each of their cases that still holds (including the histogram bounds for a won record with a try count out of range) moves into a fold case. `sync.test.ts` stops importing `applyDailyFinish` and `emptyStats`.
- `merge.test.ts`: `late` merge (on-day wins, absent counts as on-day including absent with `true` giving `false`, absent only when both absent, commutative and idempotent across all combinations of absent, `true`, `false` and several picks); the old `foldStats` cases move to `stats.test.ts`.
- `utc.test.ts`: `isLate` (same day, one day later, earlier). `guess.test.ts`: `applyCommittedGuess` sets `late` on a new record and carries the stored `late` (true, false, absent) on every later try. With the Daily-screen test, these are the only coverage of the first-try rule, because a late first try needs the Archive (#190).
- `storage.test.ts`: `late` validation; the removed accessors leave; `applySwitchOver` (marks old records, discards new unmarked ones and their pending entries, keeps marked ones, removes stats keys, idempotent, leaves invalid values alone).
- `sync.test.ts`: `late` pushed and pulled; a signed-out record's `late` reaches the account at sign-in; a pulled null reads as on-day; a record that differs only in `late` is written; no stats keys written after a pull; `createSync` takes no today.
- `remote` mapper tests (`supabase.test.ts` or a rows test): `late` maps both ways; null maps to absent.
- App-level (`mixed-daily.test.tsx` and the Reveal tests): the first committed try stores `late: false` on today's record; a stored record's `late` is unchanged by later tries; the Reveal shows stats folded from saved records (an unfinished on-day record before server today shows streak 0); no stats key is written; a late record in the store counts in Played but not in Streak.
- Existing tests that read or write the removed keys are updated to the fold behavior.
- SQL: `dailies-late.sql` as above.
