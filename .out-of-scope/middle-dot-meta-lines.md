# Middle-dot meta lines

Short meta lines join their parts with a middle dot (·):

- the Daily meta line: "#68 · 2026-11-15"
- the Codex line under the heading: "Seen 0 / 74 · Correct 0"
- each History row: "#68 · Won 3/6 · DHCP 67"

The Hallmark design skill flags middle-dot meta strings as a sign of generated design (its rule 63). Portdle keeps them.

## Why this is out of scope

The format is short and clear, and players of daily games know it. The alternatives cost space without helping the player: words such as "Puzzle 68, 15 November 2026" make the line longer on a phone, and a second line pushes the board down.

The sentence-case change for small labels (#342) changes letter case and fonts only. It does not change these separators.
